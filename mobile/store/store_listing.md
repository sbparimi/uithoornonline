## English (en-GB / en-US)

### Short description (max 80 chars)
Find trusted local pros in Uithoorn & De Kwakel. Request help for free.

### Full description (max 4000 chars)
Uithoorn.online is the local platform for Uithoorn, De Kwakel and the surrounding area. Find a trusted local business or post a free request and get replies from local professionals.

FOR RESIDENTS
• Discover local businesses by category: plumbers, electricians, cleaning, gardening, childcare, hair & beauty, tutoring, IT help and much more.
• Open a business profile and call, visit the website or get directions with one tap.
• Post a free request: describe the job, choose when and how urgent, and optionally add a budget.
• We only share your request with matching, checked businesses. Your email and phone number stay private.
• Follow your requests and chat directly with businesses that reply.

FOR BUSINESSES
• Register your business for free, in 25+ categories or with your own description.
• Receive requests from nearby customers that match your business.
• Reply with one tap and start a conversation with the customer straight away.
• Keep your business profile up to date: description, phone, website, address.

WHY UITHOORN.ONLINE
• Truly local: made for Uithoorn and De Kwakel.
• Free for residents, no obligations.
• Your data is never sold.
• Available in Dutch and English.

Questions or ideas? Email us at sam@uithoorn.online.

## Data safety form (answers to give in Play Console)
- Data collected: name, email address, phone number (optional for residents), user-generated content
  (requests, messages, business profile), approximate location only as postcode typed by the user.
- Purpose: app functionality, account management, communication between residents and businesses.
- Shared with third parties: no (requests are shown to matched businesses on the platform itself;
  service providers process data on our behalf: hosting, database, email delivery).
- Encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS). Users can request deletion: yes (in the website account settings or
  via sam@uithoorn.online); in-app: Account → Delete account opens the deletion page.
- No ads, no tracking SDKs, no payments in the app.
- Content rating questionnaire: user-generated content and user-to-user communication: yes (moderated).
- Target audience: 18+.
