# Uithoorn.online — Market Positioning

## Category
Local discovery and action platform for Uithoorn and De Kwakel.

## Core promise
Help residents move from a local need to a useful next action: discover relevant local providers, activities, food or places, then contact, visit, book, order or request.

## Consumer proposition
**Vind wat je lokaal nodig hebt.**

Ontdek betrouwbare lokale aanbieders, activiteiten en eten in Uithoorn en De Kwakel — en kom direct in actie.

## Product loop
1. Zoek — describe what you need locally.
2. Ontdek — find relevant local options.
3. Vertrouw — use clear verified information where applicable.
4. Kom in actie — call, visit, book, order or request contact.

## Differentiation
Uithoorn.online is not positioned as a news site, municipal information portal, generic business directory or broad local media platform. Its territory is local intent: helping a resident turn a need into a local action.

## Primary pillars
- Local services
- Activities and workshops
- Food and drink
- Local businesses and places

## Trust rules
- Public provider listings must come from active, verified provider records.
- Never present placeholder, sample or scraped businesses as verified marketplace inventory.
- Never invent ratings, reviews, availability, opening hours or business claims.
- Empty supply is preferable to misleading supply.

## Business proposition
**Word gevonden wanneer mensen lokaal zoeken.**

Providers should be able to create a clear local profile and receive measurable local discovery and action: contacts, requests, bookings, orders or visits.

## Strategic metric
The primary product funnel is:

**Local search → relevant discovery → provider/experience view → action**

Listing count and page views are secondary metrics.
